Platinum Road: an offline guide and tracker for all 55 GTA VI PlayStation 5 trophies — a searchable checklist with saved progress, per-trophy counters and notes, a 6-stage roadmap to the platinum, a missables view, a Next-up planner, a Sources &amp; audit view, spoiler mode, dark theme, and progress export/import. Every guide is written in our own words from the official descriptions and names 10 sources beside it (the official list on Exophase, PSNProfiles, and the published launch-day guides: PowerPyx, GTABase, Push Square, KeenGamer, GTA Spotter, eGamers, TrueAchievements); 239 cited steps in total. The audit view shows how the list was verified, the platinum roadmap facts (4/10, ~200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies, no mission replay), and what is still unverified — including the correction that only one trophy, Positive Mental Attitude, is confirmed missable by the published guides. Where a place or step is not pinned down by the official description, the guide says so instead of inventing it.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
