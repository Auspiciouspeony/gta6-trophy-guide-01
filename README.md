Platinum Road is an offline guide and tracker for all 55 GTA VI PlayStation 5 trophies. It gives you a searchable checklist with saved progress, per-trophy counters and notes, a six-stage roadmap to the platinum, a missables page, a Next-up planner, a Sources &amp; audit page, spoiler mode, a dark theme, and progress export and import. The checklist shows how many trophies match your filters, the guide pane lets you mark a trophy earned and step to the next guide without hunting for the checkbox, and the missables page lists the 14 story-locked trophies in first-pass order so you know what to do before its moment passes.

Every guide is written for this entry in our own words from the official descriptions and names 10 sources beside it — the official list on Exophase, PSNProfiles, and the published launch-day guides (PowerPyx, GTABase, Push Square, KeenGamer, GTA Spotter, eGamers, TrueAchievements) — 239 cited steps in total. The audit page shows how the list was verified, the platinum roadmap facts (4/10, ~200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies, no mission replay), and what is still unverified, including the correction that only one trophy, Positive Mental Attitude, is confirmed missable by the published guides. Where a place or step is not pinned down by the official description, the guide says so instead of inventing it.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
